# Animal Cosmetic Genes

Give the animals of your colony a new coat colour, using the colour of a gene. A RimWorld mod for the latest game version, in English and French.

## What it does

Every hair and skin colour gene of Biotech, and of a few gene mods, becomes a cosmetic trait for an animal (241 coat tints, generated). A serum bench breaks a genepack down into serums, one per gene that gives a trait. A colonist applies a serum to an animal: its coat takes a 40 % tint of the gene colour, and its young inherit the trait. A white chest patch is also available for Kuru, the black cat of Let's Have a Cat! Continued. Animals do not become sapient and nothing but their look changes.

## Requirements

- Animal Traits System (`luved.animaltraits`), which draws, restricts and inherits the traits.
- Biotech.
- Optional: Let's Have a Cat! Continued, only for the chest patch.

## Repository
