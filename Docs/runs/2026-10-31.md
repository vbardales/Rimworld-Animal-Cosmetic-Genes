Question: does a `HediffDef` with `skinColorTint` or `skinColorOverride` recolour an animal body in game?

- **Yes, on all four.** The step "the animal has the hediff" worked on its first run, and `forceRenderTreeRecache` redrew the animal at once.

- **Override**: flat, more saturated red; the sprite shading stays too. No painted marking was visible on these four animals from behind, so the loss of markings the override could cause is not shown here.
- Not tested: eyes, markings, a facing other than the spawn one (the animals were seen from behind), subtle strengths, a coat with painted markings, two hediffs at once, the real ATS trait.
